How to join

Get the game

Enemy Territory is free. Grab ET: Legacy from etlegacy.com — it is the maintained client and works on modern Windows, macOS and Linux.

Add the server

In game, open Multiplayer, then Favorites, then Add Favorite, and paste 77.126.6.231:27960.

Or connect directly

Drop the console with ~ and type /connect 77.126.6.231:27960. The Connect button at the top of this page does the same thing if your system knows the et:// link.

Pick a side

Choose Allies or Axis, pick a class, and play the objective. Bots hold the line when the server is quiet, so there is always a game running.
